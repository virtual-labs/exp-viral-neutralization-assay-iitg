### About the Experiment 

The virus neutralization assay evaluates the presence and effectiveness of virus-specific neutralizing antibodies in a given serum sample by assessing their ability to inhibit viral infection in susceptible cell cultures. It is widely used in vaccine studies, sero-surveillance, and evaluating immune responses following infection. Neutralizing antibodies specifically bind to outer viral surface glycoproteins or capsids to block host cell entry, rendering the virus non-infectious.
